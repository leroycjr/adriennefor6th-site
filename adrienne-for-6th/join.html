There Is a Place for You

Whether you have one hour a month or want to volunteer every week, your support matters. Together, we're building a campaign rooted in service, community, and opportunity for every resident of the 6th Ward.

How You Can Help

Every Talent Can Make a Difference

You don't need campaign experience. Whether you enjoy meeting neighbors, organizing events, or sharing your professional skills, there's a role for you on Team Adrienne.

Reach Our Neighbors

Connect directly with residents by circulating petitions, knocking on doors, and attending neighborhood events.

Share Your Skills

Lend your talents in photography, fundraising, social media, technology, graphic design, accounting, or legal support.

Support the Team

Help with campaign events, volunteer coordination, setup, hospitality, and behind-the-scenes logistics.

Every Great Campaign Starts With Great People

This campaign is powered by people who believe in stronger neighborhoods, greater opportunity, and responsive leadership. We'd love to welcome you to Team Adrienne.
