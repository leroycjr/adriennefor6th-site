Our Vision

Opportunity Should Reach Every Block

The 6th Ward already has the talent, history, creativity, and determined residents needed to thrive. Our next chapter requires leadership that connects those strengths to responsive government, strategic investment, and real opportunity — a ward where residents are heard, city services respond, businesses grow, families thrive, and every neighborhood has a voice in shaping its future.

How Adrienne Will Lead

Leadership That Connects People to Results

Four commitments, in the order the work actually happens — from the first conversation to measurable progress.

Listen

Residents should shape the priorities of their ward. Adrienne will create regular opportunities for residents, block clubs, businesses, faith communities, and neighborhood organizations to be heard.

Connect

Adrienne will connect the 6th Ward to resources available through city departments, financial institutions, corporations, universities, nonprofits, and community partners.

Organize

The ward office will run on clear systems — for constituent requests, community communication, project updates, and follow-through.

Deliver

Residents deserve honest answers, clear next steps, regular updates, and measurable progress.

Partnership in Practice

This Is What Bringing People to the Table Looks Like

When institutions show up in our neighborhoods — not just for meetings downtown — relationships form, investments follow, and local businesses gain customers and champions. This is the model Adrienne will bring to the ward office.

Three Priorities

A Vision Rooted in Opportunity

Create opportunity. Connect residents to resources. Restore pride in every neighborhood. These three priorities guide every decision and every partnership.

Educate

Connect residents to better jobs, workforce training, apprenticeships, business support, and access to capital — so every family has the opportunity to grow and succeed.

Focus Areas

  • Jobs & Workforce Development
  • Small Business Growth
  • Commercial Corridors

Create Access

Make the alderwoman's office the trusted place where residents can easily access programs, services, workshops, and information that improve everyday life.

Focus Areas

  • Resource Navigation
  • Community Education
  • Family & Senior Services

Reclaim Our Legacy

Restore pride in the 6th Ward through cleaner blocks, stronger neighborhoods, responsive city services, and investment that strengthens every community.

Focus Areas

  • Beautification
  • Neighborhood Investment
  • Strong Families

Building a Stronger 6th Ward Together

This campaign brings together neighbors who believe in responsive leadership, stronger connections, and greater opportunity throughout the 6th Ward.
